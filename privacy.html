Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Junaid Academy's website is a static informational site. We collect as little data as possible, and because we serve families with children, we hold ourselves to a cautious standard.

What we collect

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts. The waitlist form does not transmit data anywhere until enrollment opens; nothing you type leaves your browser. We never knowingly collect information directly from children — waitlist signups are for parents and guardians.

Hosting

The site is served by our hosting provider, which may log standard technical request data (IP address, user agent) to operate and secure the service.

When enrollment opens

Before any form on this site transmits data, this policy will be updated to describe exactly what is collected, why, and how it is protected.
